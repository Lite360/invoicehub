Build reusable components instead of duplicating invoice, quotation and receipt logic.

Create clean TypeScript types.

Use proper error handling.

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

InvoiceHub is a SaaS platform that enables businesses to create professional invoices, quotations, receipts and business letters.

Businesses can customize their documents using their own logo, colors, letterhead, watermark and digital signature.

The platform also provides customer management, payment collection through Paystack, automated receipts, email delivery through Resend, subscription billing and document history.

InvoiceHub is designed as a responsive Progressive Web App that can be installed on Android and iOS devices.

2. PROBLEM

Many small businesses create invoices and quotations manually using Word, Excel, Canva or generic templates.

This creates several problems:

Inconsistent branding
Manual calculations
Poor document organization
Difficult payment tracking
No centralized customer history
Manual receipt generation
Difficult document delivery
No professional digital workflow
Limited mobile experience

InvoiceHub solves these problems in one platform.
